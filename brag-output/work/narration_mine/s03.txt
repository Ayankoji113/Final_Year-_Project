Microservices turned rest A.P.I.'s into the front line. The usual defences, web application firewalls and static rate limiters, only catch what they already have a signature for. And during traffic spikes, they often block the very users they're meant to protect. Micro A.P.I. Guard combines both worlds: deterministic rules for what is certain, and unsupervised models, trained only on normal traffic, for what no signature anticipates.
