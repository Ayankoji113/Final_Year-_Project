Everything that survives layer one becomes a vector of thirty-four behavioural features: the shape of the body, the path, and the query. Their length, depth, entropy, and special characters, and how much decoding changed them. Plus endpoint breadth, the H.T.T.P. method, and the number of flag hits. Just as important is what is missing. There is no endpoint identity, so the model learns what a request looks like, not where it went. That is what lets it sit in front of a different backend. And there are no client headers, like the user agent. An attacker copies those for free, and they were the single biggest source of false positives.
