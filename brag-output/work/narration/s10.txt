Layers two and three are unsupervised. They are trained only on normal traffic, so they never need attack labels. The Isolation Forest, three hundred trees, measures how easily a request can be isolated from the crowd. The autoencoder, written in pure Num Pie, with no Pie Torch, squeezes the thirty-four features through a narrow bottleneck, and tries to rebuild them. Normal traffic reconstructs cleanly. Anomalies do not, and the reconstruction error becomes a score.
