Here is the shape of it. Clients reach the gateway on port five thousand, the only port that is published. Behind it, on an internal network, sit the backend and Reddis, deliberately unreachable from the host, so the gateway cannot be bypassed. Every request flows down one pipeline: normalization, layer one rules and rate limits, feature extraction, an Isolation Forest, an autoencoder, and finally, a gradient-boosted meta-learner. Fast A P I, Reddis, sci kit learn, and Num Pie, all running in Docker.
