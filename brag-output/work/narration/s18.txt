Early on, the ensemble scored an F one of zero point nine nine. It looked perfect. But the test data came from the same traffic generator used for training, and that generator always called each endpoint the same way. The model had learned how each endpoint is normally called, not what an attack is. A bare get, on the products endpoint, scored zero point nine nine: attack. On traffic from a second, independent client, it was close to chance, with a R O C A U C of zero point five six. Finding that, and fixing it, became the main contribution of this work.
